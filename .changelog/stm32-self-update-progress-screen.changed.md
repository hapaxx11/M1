STM32 (Hapax) self firmware-update now shows its progress on a dedicated full-screen view — title, "Do not power off" warning, a centered "Erasing flash..." status during the erase phase, and a clean framed percentage bar — instead of drawing the old "Update progress: NN %" slider strip over the menu. This matches the ESP32 update screen. Progress math extracted to `fw_update_progress_percent()` in `m1_fw_progress.h` with regression tests in `test_fw_progress.c`.
