**Games: Flappy Bird, Coin Flip, Rock Paper Scissors, Tamagotchi** — four new
  built-in games added to the Games menu, ported from bedge117/M1 ("C3"):
  Flappy Bird (OK/UP to flap, pipe collision + best-score tracking), Coin
  Flip (animated spin with running heads/tails stats), Rock Paper Scissors
  (LEFT/RIGHT to choose, OK to play against the CPU, score tracking), and
  Tamagotchi P1 (native pet simulation with the classic eight-icon
  Food/Light/Game/Medicine/Clean/Status/Discipline/Attention menu, stat
  decay, evolution, and SD-card auto-save/load via `0:/Games/tama.dat`).
